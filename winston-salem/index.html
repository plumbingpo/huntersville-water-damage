Typical Water Damage Problems in Winston-Salem

Most water damage calls we take in Winston-Salem trace back to a short list of causes. Knowing them makes it easier to catch trouble while it is still a small repair.

Pipes: When water freezes inside a line it can crack the metal or plastic, then pour out as soon as things warm up. Lines in exterior walls, garages and unheated attics are the usual trouble spots, depending on the season. Corroded fittings and worn supply lines can also let go without any warning in older homes. Shut off the main valve right away, because an open line can soak several rooms quickly.

Appliances: The thin hoses feeding a washer, dishwasher or ice maker do not last forever. Tanks rust from the inside, so an old water heater may drip for weeks or give out suddenly. These failures do the most harm when the appliance is on an upper floor or above finished living space.

Overflows: A clogged toilet or a sink left running can put water on the floor in minutes. The water often runs under baseboards and through the ceiling of the room below. If the overflow came from the toilet bowl or a drain, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away.

Roof: Missing shingles, cracked flashing and clogged gutters let rain into the attic. The stain on your ceiling usually shows up well after the insulation above it is soaked. We dry the framing, insulation and drywall, and a roofer needs to fix the opening so it does not return.

Basement: In homes that have a basement, groundwater pushes through foundation cracks and floor joints when the soil is saturated. A sump pump that loses power or jams during a heavy rain lets the pit overflow. Finished basements suffer most, since carpet, drywall and framing all soak up water from the bottom.

Mold: Damp drywall, wood and carpet can begin to grow mold in as little as 24 to 48 hours. A lot of the mold we find in Winston-Salem comes from a past leak that looked dry on the surface only. A musty smell, peeling paint or dark spotting near a previous repair is worth having checked.
