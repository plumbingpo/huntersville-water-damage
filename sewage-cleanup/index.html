Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Huntersville, Huntersville Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
